## Arming sword

`models/arming_sword.glb` (European arming sword, c. 1400) was made for this project: the mesh is script-generated in Blender and its three textures (base colour, normal, occlusion/roughness/metallic) are painted procedurally (self-made). It draws on:

- Brown Leather (`brown_leather`) by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0: grip leather colour, roughness and grain
- Rusty Metal 02 (`rusty_metal_02`) by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven, CC0: diffuse map used only as a mask for patina spots
- Proportion references (photographs not included in the model): The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access (CC0), [Sword, 1375–1450, acc. 32.75.226](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/32238) and [Sword, ca. 1400, acc. 32.75.225](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/23367)
